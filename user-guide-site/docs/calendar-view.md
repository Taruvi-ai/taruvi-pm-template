# Calendar View

Calendar View helps you understand when work is scheduled, not just what exists.

<img src="/user-guide/img/user-guide/calendar.png" alt="Calendar view" />

## Ways to look at time

Use the controls at the top of the page to switch between:

- `Month` for a broad schedule overview.
- `Week` for near-term planning.
- `Full Year` for long-range visibility.

## Useful filters

- `Tasks Assigned to Me` keeps the calendar focused on your own work.
- `Project` narrows the timeline to one project.
- Department and team filters help when you are coordinating group work.

## Common uses

- Check what is due this week.
- Look for schedule collisions.
- Review task timing before meetings.
- Jump into a task from its calendar entry to update details.

:::note Calendar colors
Calendar entry colors are based on the project color. They do not show task status or priority by themselves.
:::

:::note No dates, no timeline
Tasks without start or end dates do not appear on the calendar timeline. Add dates to make planning views more useful.
:::
