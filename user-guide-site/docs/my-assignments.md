# My Assignments

My Assignments is the fastest way to focus on work that belongs to you.

<img src="/user-guide/img/user-guide/assignments.png" alt="My Assignments" />

## What this screen is for

Use My Assignments when you want to:

- See only tasks assigned to you.
- Filter work by project.
- Switch between list and board views.
- Hide completed tasks so the view stays focused.

:::note Why a task may still appear here
You may see a task in My Assignments even when you do not have normal access to the full project through private, group, team, or department settings. Assignment can place work in your queue even if the project itself is not part of your usual project list.
:::

## Filters that matter most

- `Project` helps you narrow your work to a single project.
- `Priority` helps you spot urgent work first.
- `Sort` and `Order` help you decide whether to focus on newest or oldest items.
- `Hide Done` keeps the screen centered on active work.

## Views available here

- `List view` is better for reading details and opening tasks one by one.
- `Board view` is better when you want to scan status lanes quickly.

## Add work from your queue

Use `Add Task` when a new task needs to be created while you are already working from the assignments screen.
